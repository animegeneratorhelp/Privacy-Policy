This Privacy Policy describes how Anime Collection RPG handles user data.
The Application does not require registration, collect, store, or share any personal information. It operates fully on-device without identifying users.

By installing or using the Application, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

**1. Data Collection and Processing**
The Application does not collect or process any personally identifiable information (PII), such as:

Name, email address, or contact details

Account credentials

IP addresses or unique device identifiers

There is no registration or login system, and no accounts are created or stored.

**2. AI Generation Data (Prompts)**

Users can enter text prompts to generate AI-based anime characters and artworks.
These prompts are used only for real-time image generation and are not stored, logged, or linked to user identity.

The Application does not upload or save user images, biometric data, or any personal media.

**3. In-App Purchases**

All in-app purchases are processed securely through the Apple App Store and Google Play Billing systems.
The Application does not access, store, or transmit any payment data.

To verify purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which may utilize an anonymous device identifier for validation only.
RevenueCat does not collect personal identity information.
All billing data is handled solely by Apple or Google according to their privacy policies.

**4. Device Permissions**

The Application may request access to the photo gallery to allow users to save generated images locally on their devices.
This permission is used exclusively for saving images — never for tracking, analytics, or data collection.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

**The Application:**

Does not share data with third parties for analytics, advertising, or tracking

Does not sell, rent, or transfer personal data

Does not engage in profiling or cross-platform identification

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to disclose or share.

**6. Children’s Privacy**

The Application is not directed toward children and does not knowingly collect data from minors.
Since no personal information is gathered, no data relating to children is stored.

**7. Security**

As the Application does not collect or store personal data, no personal information can be exposed or compromised.
All generated content is saved locally and remains under the user’s control.

**8. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any new version will replace the previous one once published within the app or on our website.
